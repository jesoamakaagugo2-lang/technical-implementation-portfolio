How I approach implementation

Work sample · Illustrative

Office phone system cutover

A sample rollout plan for a small business moving to a new IP phone system. It covers requirements, call-flow testing and a fallback if the change fails.

Read the cutover plan
  1. Confirm requirements. List extensions, numbers, business hours and fallback contacts. Map the main number to the greeting, ring group and voicemail.
  2. Configure and test. Verify SIP registration, then test inbound and outbound calls, transfers, two-way audio, busy and no-answer handling, and after-hours routing.
  3. Prepare the change. Record the prior routing, agree on the go-live window and confirm who can restore the previous route if incoming calls fail.
  4. Hand over. Record pass or fail results, open issues and ownership. Give the customer a quick guide for transfers, voicemail and reporting problems.

Work sample · Illustrative

Isolating an outbound call failure

A sample diagnostic sequence for an extension that receives calls but cannot dial out.

Read the troubleshooting sequence
  1. Establish scope. Test a known-good extension with the same destination and dial format. Record the time, symptom and any SIP response.
  2. Check the route. Compare outbound permissions and dial rules. If multiple extensions fail, check trunk status and provider responses before changing extension settings.
  3. Verify and hand over. Retest the original scenario, confirm inbound calls still work and record the result. If the issue persists, hand over the test evidence and next owner.
